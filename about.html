About The Golden Glaze Bakery

The Golden Glaze Bakery was established in 2021 after Khamila Rocher baked cinnamon rolls for a family celebration.

The cinnamon rolls were enjoyed by the guests, who placed orders on the same day. This inspired Khamila to turn her passion for baking into a business.

Our Mission

To create irresistible cinnamon rolls that make every bite worth coming back for.

Our Vision

To grow into a successful bakery, known for delicious cinnamon rolls, creativity, quality, and reliable service.

Our Target Audience

Our target audience includes students, young adults, families and sweet-tooth customers looking for affordable, fresh and delicious cinnamon rolls.
